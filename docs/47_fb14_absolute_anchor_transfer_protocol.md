# FB14 — absolute-anchor signed-positive transfer protocol

Status: **selection and scoring specified before FB14 E1 or normal outcomes were examined**. This is a separate, local prospective transfer check of the unchanged FB08 gate, not a new independently timestamped preregistration and not a new training set. The raw official annotation file had already been downloaded and audited for structure in FB01; its two multi-point collections, geometry, and label distribution were inspected to design this protocol. No E1 or normal-field result on these pairs was inspected before this text and the tracked freeze were written.

## Scientific question

Can the frozen FB08 normal-applicability and E1-confidence gate retain accurate, signed nonzero winding proposals on a **different human-annotated positive source**, rather than only rejecting constructed zero cues? A poor result is as important to report as a good one. This is not a fitter benchmark.

## Source, frame, and population

Use the pinned official Paris 4 [`abs_winding.json`](https://dl.ash2txt.org/datasets/spiral_datasets/PHercParis4/abs_winding.json), SHA-256 `4e566731f7cbaf8f5ec843de687b3f72f4a784c40b587ebbaf544550902172c1`. Only collections with `metadata.winding_is_absolute == true` and at least two finite, integer-valued `wind_a` points are eligible. The public point `p` is interpreted as xyz **at the 9.6 µm working/L2 frame**, as in the FB08 relative annotation source; do not divide it by four. The field and normal group-4 arrays are then sampled at `p[::-1]/4`. This remains a frame hypothesis supported by metadata and CT anchors, not independently verified registration.

Within each eligible collection, enumerate every unordered distinct point pair sorted by numeric point ID. Retain pairs with finite Euclidean chord length in `[10, 50]` working voxels, `|Δz| < 1` working voxel, and nonzero absolute-label difference. Do not pair across collections or use E1/normal values in selection. To prevent all sign labels from following point-ID order, orient each retained pair by the low bit of SHA-256 on the UTF-8 string `FB14-absolute-pair-v1:{collection_id}:{low_point_id}:{high_point_id}`: bit zero keeps low→high, bit one flips it. `truth_dw = wind_a(b) - wind_a(a)`. This orientation is fixed without looking at E1 output. The manifest stores source and candidate hashes, coordinates, labels, collection IDs, and endpoint IDs under ignored `artifacts/`.

The source contains 59 points across six collections, but only collections 1 and 5 have more than one point. The geometry-only candidate screen yields **90 within-collection pairs** (84 and 6), with absolute differences 1:51, 2:26, 3:9, 4:4. These pairs overlap heavily in endpoints and are **not 90 independent human decisions**. A geometry-only nearest-neighbor check placed every absolute-anchor point at least about 115 working voxels from the existing FB08 mixed-candidate endpoints; this is a spatially separate source, not a new scan. Report both collections separately, not just a pooled metric.

## Locked scorer and comparisons

Apply the exact FB08 E1 v1_5 implementation and `decide_relative_constraint` source SHA pinned in `protocols/FB08_mixed_constraint_gate_freeze.json`: seven rays; nonzero E1 answer; both endpoint normals valid; minimum absolute chord–normal dot at both endpoints ≥0.75; E1 confidence ≥0.75. Keep `registration_verified=false`, so a numerically eligible edge remains `review`, never production `accept`. Reuse the same nearest-integer normal probe used in FB08; do not describe it as dense official fitter parity.

Primary descriptive outcome: among **all 90 candidates**, report answered count, numeric-gate emit count/coverage, exact signed count and precision among emitted, and number of wrong emitted. Also report E1 answered/nonzero exact, E1 confidence≥0.75 exact, and E1 confidence-ranked top-*N* where *N* equals the frozen gate's emit count (ID-stable tie break). Report each comparison at its own coverage and, for top-*N*, identical coverage. Report by source collection and true absolute magnitude; include all error signs/magnitudes, reason-code counts, and the zero-emit case explicitly. A constant-label baseline must be stated in relation to the sign-balanced candidate set; do not present a high score without it. Do not tune a threshold, normal decoder, pair rule, or orientation from this outcome. The pairwise dependence and only two source clusters prohibit a meaningful collection-bootstrap generalization interval.

## Provenance, interpretation, and stop rule

Hash-link the source, candidate manifest, exact normal/gradient plans, acquired ranges, FB08 freeze/gate source, and scored result. Exact HTTP 206 ranges and byte hashes remain ignored; do not redistribute raw annotations or field bytes. If the source frame or normal/gradient support fails, report the failure without changing the pair population. If the gate performs poorly, document the mechanism and do not promote a positive-generalization claim. If it performs well, the permitted claim is *local transfer to a second official human annotation source with known dependence and frame uncertainty*—not independent proof of true CT winding, improved spiral fitting, or a production export. The abs labels may encode a single annotator's correction path, and the two positive collections cannot establish broad reliability.
